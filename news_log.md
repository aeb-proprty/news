## 2026-05-08

### DANISH POLITICS: Coalition Talks Break National Record — Day 44 and Counting
*Continuation from April 7 — the negotiations you read about have now become the longest in Danish history.* On May 6, talks passed the 42-day record set in 2022. Frederiksen continues meeting party leaders at Marienborg. The likeliest government shape is Social Democrats + SF + Moderates + Radikale Venstre, with external support from Enhedslisten and Alternativet. However, the Moderates reportedly prefer pulling in Venstre and Konservative instead. Løkke Rasmussen remains the kingmaker. No breakthrough appears imminent.

**Life Impact: HIGH** — Denmark has been without a real government for 44 days. Policy on housing, defense spending, and green transition is on hold until this resolves. Any coalition configuration will directly affect Copenhagen housing regulation and tax policy.

### GEOPOLITICS: Trump-Xi Summit Confirmed for May 14-15 in Beijing
Trump travels to Beijing next week for the first US-China summit of his second term. Originally planned for March, it was postponed due to the Iran conflict. The agenda is dominated by Iran (Trump wants China to pressure Tehran), but Taiwan, tariffs, and rare earth export controls are also on the table. China is pressing Iran to reopen the Strait of Hormuz ahead of the summit. A reciprocal Xi visit to the White House is planned later this year.

**Life Impact: MEDIUM-HIGH** — The summit outcome will directly affect global trade tensions, energy prices, and the EU tariff situation. If tariff de-escalation happens, it relieves pressure on EU export industries including Danish pharma and manufacturing.

### GEOPOLITICS: US-Iran War Day 69 — Ceasefire Memo "Close" but Fragile
*Continuation from April 7 (Day 38).* A one-page memorandum to formally end the war is reportedly close. Under the draft: Iran halts uranium enrichment for 12 years, the US lifts sanctions and unfreezes assets, and the Strait of Hormuz reopens within 30 days. Trump says talks are "very positive." However, Iran accuses the US of violating the existing ceasefire by targeting vessels, Israel bombed Beirut on May 7 (first strike since the April 17 ceasefire), and IRGC warns the conflict could resume. US gas prices have hit $4.46/gallon — highest in nearly four years.

**Life Impact: HIGH** — Strait of Hormuz closure continues to affect global oil prices. A deal would stabilize energy costs; a collapse would spike them further. European energy prices track this closely.

### 3D PRINTING: Japan's First 3D-Printed Two-Story Home; Granule-Based FDM Printing Advances
Startup Kizuki unveiled Japan's first 3D-printed two-story house — 50m², 6 meters tall, printed on-site in 14 days, and built to Japan's strict seismic codes. In desktop printing, the Direct Granules Extruder V7.0 is maturing, allowing FDM printing directly from pellets instead of filament (cheaper materials, no spool management). Chinese company Eplus3D broke the 3-meter barrier in metal powder bed fusion with 256 lasers — relevant for industrial/aerospace manufacturing.

**Life Impact: LOW-MEDIUM** — The granule extruder is the most immediately interesting for hobbyists: cheaper materials and more exotic material options. The construction printing story signals where housing construction is heading.
