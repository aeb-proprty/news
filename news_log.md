### DANISH POLITICS: Frederiksen's Coalition Talks Collapse — Liberal Leader Gets 2 Weeks
After record-breaking 7 weeks of failed negotiations following the March 24 snap election, Mette Frederiksen's coalition talks officially broke down on May 8. Venstre leader Troels Lund Poulsen was handed the mandate on Friday and has two weeks to form a government. He's meeting all parties Monday-Tuesday. Frederiksen may still emerge as PM if Poulsen fails. This is now the longest government formation in Danish history.
