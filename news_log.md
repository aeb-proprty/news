### DANISH POLITICS: Coalition Deadlock Deepens — Both Frederiksen and Poulsen Fail
**Update from April 7:** Coalition talks have now collapsed twice. Frederiksen's attempt failed in early May, and King Frederik handed the task to Venstre leader Troels Lund Poulsen. Poulsen tried to form a centre-right government (Venstre + Liberal Alliance + Conservatives) but Lars Lokke Rasmussen's Moderates torpedoed it by insisting on inclusion and a broad centrist government — neither of which Poulsen was offering. Poulsen's talks officially broke down yesterday (May 22). Denmark has now been without a government for 60 days since the March 24 election. King Frederik will hold fresh talks with party leaders — Frederiksen could get another shot, or a third candidate could emerge. The 12-party fragmentation makes any majority extremely difficult.

**Life Impact: HIGH** — Two months without a government means policy paralysis on housing, defense spending, and Greenland strategy. As a Venstre member, the party's path to power just hit a wall. The longer the deadlock, the louder calls for a new election.

### DANISH/EU POLITICS: Push to Anchor Greenland in European Structures
Europabevægelsen's latest newsletter highlights growing momentum to bind Greenland more tightly into European political and security frameworks. The argument: stronger EU political engagement, increased funding, closer participation in EU programmes, and potentially a European military presence to monitor the GIUK gap and counter Russian/Chinese Arctic encroachment. The goal is to make Greenland's European anchoring so deep that external pressure (read: Trump) becomes structurally harder to exert. This shifts from the January "solidarity statements" phase to concrete institutional proposals.

**Life Impact: MEDIUM-HIGH** — Denmark's defense spending and Arctic posture will be shaped by this debate. If Greenland integration deepens, expect higher Danish defense budgets and new EU Arctic cooperation frameworks.

### EU POLITICS: Parliament Passes Mandatory Foreign Investment Screening
The European Parliament voted 508-64 to approve mandatory FDI screening for strategic sectors: defense, semiconductors, AI, critical raw materials, quantum technologies, and financial services. Crucially, the rules also cover intra-EU investments by companies ultimately owned by third-country investors. Still needs formal Council approval, then 18 months to implementation. All member states will be required to have screening mechanisms and notify the Commission of risky cross-border investment cases.

**Life Impact: MEDIUM-HIGH** — If you work in PropTech or AI in the EU, foreign investment into your sector now faces a new regulatory layer. For the broader Danish tech/startup scene, this could slow some funding rounds but protects strategic sectors.

### IRAN-US CONFLICT: Ceasefire Holds But Brinkmanship Continues
**Update from April 7:** The hot war phase (Operation Epic Fury) ended May 5. A ceasefire agreed April 7-8 has broadly held. The conflict has shifted to a dangerous game over the Strait of Hormuz — Iran still restricts access, and negotiations mediated by Pakistan cover freedom of navigation, Iran's nuclear programme, reconstruction, sanctions, and long-term peace. Iran's health ministry reports 3,468 killed and 26,500+ injured from US-Israeli strikes since February 28. Sporadic violations continue — IDF reported 4 missile salvos from Iran this week, mostly intercepted. Kuwait's ports were hit by drones causing material damage.

**Life Impact: HIGH** — The Strait of Hormuz situation directly affects energy prices. A permanent settlement would ease global oil markets; breakdown could spike them. Watch fuel and energy costs.

### AI: Novo Nordisk Partners with OpenAI — Denmark's Biggest AI Deal
Denmark's pharmaceutical giant Novo Nordisk announced a strategic partnership with OpenAI to integrate AI across its entire operation — drug discovery, clinical trials, manufacturing, supply chain, and corporate operations. Pilot programmes launching across all divisions with full integration targeted by end of 2026. The deal is part of Novo's fight to claw back market share from Eli Lilly in the weight-loss drug market (Wegovy pill launched in January). OpenAI will also help build AI fluency across Novo's global workforce. Strict data protection and human oversight governance built in.

**Life Impact: HIGH** — This is the biggest AI-pharma deal from a Danish company. It signals that major Danish corporations are going all-in on AI integration. Could accelerate drug development timelines for treatments millions depend on.

### AI: OpenAI Launches Self-Serve Ad Platform in ChatGPT
OpenAI's ChatGPT advertising platform is now fully live with a self-serve Ads Manager (as of May 5, no minimum spend required — down from the initial $50K pilot). Ads appear as labeled, tinted boxes at the bottom of AI responses on Free and Go tiers. The platform uses contextual matching based on conversation topics and history, not keyword targeting. Generated $100M revenue in its first six weeks. Paid subscribers remain ad-free.

**Life Impact: MEDIUM** — This changes the AI assistant landscape. If you use free-tier AI tools, expect ads to become standard. For anyone building AI products, this is a new monetization model to watch.
