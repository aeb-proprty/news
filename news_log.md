# Daily News Log

## 2026-07-18

### GEOPOLITICS: Trump Accuses China of 2020 Election Interference in Primetime Address
In a primetime White House speech on July 16, Trump claimed China stole 220 million voter files and that US intelligence agencies covered it up. He declassified documents to support his case. US intelligence previously concluded with "high confidence" that Beijing did not try to influence the 2020 election. China's foreign ministry called the claims groundless. The timing is politically charged: Xi Jinping's state visit to Washington is planned for late September. Markets reacted negatively — the Economist's July 18 headline reads "Global stocks sink."

**Life Impact: MEDIUM-HIGH** — This threatens the fragile US-China trade truce that has kept tariff levels manageable. If relations deteriorate again, expect supply chain disruptions and price increases on consumer electronics and goods. The semiconductor index (SOX) is already down ~20% from recent highs.

---

### GEOPOLITICS: Trump Revives Greenland Push at NATO Summit, Denmark Pledges Defense
At the NATO summit in Ankara (July 7-8), Trump reiterated that the US should control Greenland "for the protection of the world," citing missile early warning systems and transpolar shipping routes. PM Frederiksen pushed back firmly, pledging to defend "every inch" of Danish territory. Trump had backed off at Davos in January but has now resumed pressure.

**Life Impact: HIGH** — This directly affects Denmark's defense spending, foreign policy posture, and national identity debate. Frederiksen's new government was formed partly on the back of this crisis. Expect continued elevated defense budgets and potential economic friction with the US.

### AI: China Launches 29-Nation AI Alliance (WAICO) to Counter US Dominance
On July 16, Xi Jinping announced the World Artificial Intelligence Cooperation Organization (WAICO) at the Shanghai AI conference. Headquartered in Shanghai, founding members include Indonesia, Brazil, Malaysia, South Africa, Russia, and Pakistan. Xi emphasized open-source AI and pledged 5,000 AI training spots for developing nations over 5 years. He called AI development "a symphony of international cooperation, not a solo performance."

**Life Impact: MEDIUM** — This formalizes the AI geopolitical split. EU companies building AI may face increasing pressure to pick sides. For developers, it signals more open-source Chinese models entering the market (like DeepSeek's trajectory).

### AI: OpenAI Releases GPT-5.6 (Sol/Terra/Luna) and ChatGPT Work
Released July 9 after government-imposed delays, GPT-5.6 comes in three tiers: Sol (most powerful, $5/$30 per M tokens), Terra (balanced, $2.50/$15), and Luna (fast, $1/$6). Sol achieves SOTA across coding, knowledge work, and cybersecurity. ChatGPT Work launched alongside — it integrates context from your team's tools to produce finished outputs from scattered notes and drafts.

**Life Impact: MEDIUM-HIGH** — If you use AI tools professionally, GPT-5.6 Sol is the new benchmark to evaluate against. ChatGPT Work is a direct competitor to enterprise workflow tools. Pricing is aggressive — Luna at $1/M input is cheaper than most current models.

### EU: New Driver Distraction Detection Mandatory in All New Cars
Since July 7, 2026, all newly registered cars in the EU must include a driver distraction detection system that analyzes the driver's gaze and head movements. This is part of the EU General Safety Regulation's phased rollout.

**Life Impact: LOW-MEDIUM** — If you're buying a new car, it will have this system. Unlikely to affect used car purchases for years, but signals the EU's continued willingness to mandate in-vehicle tech.

### COPENHAGEN HOUSING: Nationalbanken Warns of Unsustainable 25% Price Surge
Danmarks Nationalbank flagged Copenhagen apartment prices as having surged 25% in the 12 months through April 2026 — deemed unsustainable relative to wage growth. The central bank warns that purchases are increasingly driven by expectations of further gains rather than fundamentals. Governor Nødgaard stressed that lending rules must not be eased. Average apartment price now ~75,000 DKK/m2 in the city, with central/waterfront areas far above.

**Life Impact: VERY HIGH** — If you own, your equity has grown substantially but a correction risk is now officially flagged by the central bank. If looking to buy, the Nationalbank is essentially saying current prices are expectation-driven and vulnerable to reversal. The fact that price growth is spreading beyond Copenhagen is a systemic concern.

### 3D PRINTING: Meshy Launches T2 Smart Topology Model via API
Meshy released Meshy-T2 (announced in your email July 16), a new model that turns a single image into a clean, low-poly 3D model with production-ready topology. It outputs quad or triangle meshes from 1k to 300k faces, directly usable in Blender or for 3D printing without manual retopology. This builds on January's Meshy-6 low-poly mode.

**Life Impact: LOW-MEDIUM** — Directly relevant if you use AI-to-3D workflows. The clean topology output means prints come out better without manual mesh cleanup. API access means it can be integrated into automated pipelines.

### 3D PRINTING: Kentstrapper Launches 1m3 FFF Printer; Rolls-Royce Opens AM Cell
Kentstrapper's "Mille" is a 1000x1000x1000mm FFF printer now on sale. Meanwhile, Rolls-Royce opened an additive manufacturing cell in Bristol (UK MOD funded) for next-gen aircraft engine components. UltiMaker launched the Factor 4 Plus for continuous factory production. 3D Prod acquired Sculpteo, combining prototyping and industrial production.

**Life Impact: LOW** — The Mille is interesting if you need massive build volume. The industry consolidation (Sculpteo acquisition) signals AM is maturing past hobbyist into serious manufacturing.
