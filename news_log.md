### EU: €3 Customs Duty Live, €190B Joint Defense Push, US Trade Deal Deadline Met

**New €3 customs duty** (July 1): Every parcel under €150 from outside the EU now gets a flat €3 charge per item type by tariff code. Captures ~93% of all imported e-commerce parcels. Runs until July 2028. If you buy 5 t-shirts = €3. Add a watch = €6. This killed the €150 duty-free threshold.

**Defense projects** (July 3): The Commission proposed 5 joint European Defence Projects of Common Interest (EDPCIs) covering drones/counter-drones, eastern flank, maritime/seabed, air/missile defense, and space defense. Combined ambition: **€190 billion by 2036**. Ukraine participates in 4 of 5 projects. Average 18 member states per project. Initial allocation: €325M from EDIP.

**EU-US trade deal** implemented on deadline (July 4): EU eliminated all duties on US industrial goods. US auto tariff cut from 27.5% to 15%. EU auto tariff cut from 10% to 0%. Single 15% ceiling across most sectors. Trump had threatened to hike EU car tariffs from 15% to 25% if not implemented by July 4.

**Parliament session** (July 6-9): Voting on Ukraine/Moldova EU membership progress (Cluster 6 "External Relations" opening expected July 14), air passenger rights reform (compensation 250-600€ unchanged, no-show penalties banned), Serbia accession, EU-Mexico trade.

**Ireland** took over EU Council Presidency July 1. Priorities: competitiveness, values/enlargement, security. Key negotiation: next Multiannual Financial Framework (2028-2034).

**Life Impact: HIGH** — The customs duty directly raises costs on all your non-EU online orders (3D printing parts, electronics from China, AliExpress). The EU-US trade deal means lower car prices on American imports and stabilizes transatlantic relations. The defense push signals a permanent structural shift in EU spending priorities.
