### BREAKING: Blue Origin New Glenn Rocket Explodes on Launch Pad
Last night (May 28), Blue Origin's 188-foot New Glenn rocket exploded during an engine-firing test at Cape Canaveral. The seven BE-4 engines had barely started firing when the first stage was engulfed in flames; the upper stage tilted and collapsed. No injuries. This was Blue Origin's only New Glenn pad, so the damage will significantly delay their schedule, including a planned June launch of Amazon "Leo" internet satellites and contributions to NASA's Artemis moon program.

**Life Impact: MEDIUM** — No direct personal impact, but it sets back commercial space competition and potentially delays Artemis moon missions and Amazon's satellite internet rollout. If you were watching Bezos vs. Musk in space, this is a major Musk advantage moment.

### US-IRAN: Tentative Ceasefire Extension Deal, Awaiting Trump Sign-Off
*Previously: April 7 — Iran rejected a US ceasefire, Strait of Hormuz closed, missile exchanges ongoing, Day 38 of conflict.*

The war has progressed significantly. A tentative deal has been reached to extend the ceasefire by 60 days, reopen the Strait of Hormuz, and establish a framework for nuclear talks. Key sticking points remain: Iran's uranium stockpile, frozen assets, and enrichment moratorium terms. However, US and Iranian forces exchanged fire again on May 28, and Trump says he's "not satisfied" with the terms. The MOU awaits his final approval.

**Life Impact: HIGH** — The Strait of Hormuz reopening would stabilize global oil/energy prices. If the deal collapses, expect another fuel price spike. This has been dragging on for 3+ months now.

### DANISH POLITICS: Record-Long Coalition Talks — Frederiksen Gets Second Chance
*Previously: April 7 — Post-election deadlock, no bloc with majority, coalition talks ongoing.*

The saga has taken a dramatic turn. Troels Lund Poulsen's centre-right coalition attempt collapsed on May 22 over disagreements on tax cuts, infrastructure vs. welfare spending, and the budget deficit. He informed King Frederik X, who then re-appointed Mette Frederiksen as formateur on May 23 with support from the Moderates and red-bloc parties. She is now pursuing a centre-left coalition with the Green Left. These are now the longest government formation talks in modern Danish history, breaking the 42-day record from 2022. The Greenland/Trump crisis remains a pressing background issue.

**Life Impact: VERY HIGH** — You're directly affected. A centre-left coalition with the Green Left would likely mean stricter housing/environmental regulations, higher green building standards, and potentially more tenant protections in Copenhagen. Tax policy and defense spending priorities are also at stake.

### EU POLITICS: Orbán Is Out — Magyar Wins Hungarian Election in Landslide
*Previously: April 7 — Magyar was polling neck-and-neck with Orbán.*

It happened. On April 12, Péter Magyar's Tisza Party won 141 of 199 seats (53.6% of the vote) — a supermajority. Orbán's Fidesz collapsed to 55 seats (37.8%). Turnout was 79.6%, a record. Orbán conceded on election night. This ends 16 years of Orbán rule and removes the EU's most persistent veto-blocker. EU-Ukraine aid, defense coordination, rule-of-law enforcement, and migration policy can now move forward without Hungarian obstruction.

**Life Impact: HIGH** — This reshapes EU decision-making. Expect faster progress on EU defense integration, Ukraine support, and possibly new migration frameworks. Denmark's voice in the EU gains weight as the blocking minority shrinks.

### EU: €860B Defense Plan Fully Subscribed, Freezing Out US Contractors
Europe's SAFE loan instrument (€150B) has been fully subscribed by 19 member states. The broader €860B defense spending plan explicitly favors European manufacturers over US contractors. Germany alone plans €117B in 2026 defense spending, rising to €162B by 2029. France is at €68.5B. EU-wide defense spending hit 2.1% of GDP in 2025 and is climbing. Denmark will need to decide how it positions its defense industry and procurement in this new landscape.

**Life Impact: MEDIUM** — Increased defense spending across Europe means higher government budgets, which could crowd out other spending. But it also means European defense industry jobs and less dependence on US military equipment. Denmark's upcoming government will need to make big procurement decisions.

### 3D PRINTING: Bioprinted Trachea Successfully Transplanted, Recyclable Resin Developed
Two notable developments: (1) A 3D-printed trachea transplanted by Prof. Kim Seong-won's team in South Korea showed full healing with new blood vessel growth at 6-month follow-up — a medical first.
