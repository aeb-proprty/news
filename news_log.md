## 2026-05-30

### DANISH POLITICS (cont'd): Centre-Right Collapses — Frederiksen Gets Second Shot
The centre-right coalition attempt led by Troels Lund Poulsen collapsed on May 22 over disputes on tax cuts, infrastructure vs. welfare spending, and the budget deficit. King Frederik asked Frederiksen on May 23 to try again. These are now the longest coalition talks in Danish history (67+ days), beating the 42-day record from 2022. The key obstacle remains Lars Løkke Rasmussen's Moderates — analysts expect he'll demand major policy concessions. Denmark remains in caretaker government with no resolution in sight.

**Life Impact: VERY HIGH** — Caretaker government means no new legislation on housing, tax, or defense. The longer this drags, the more policy uncertainty affects property decisions and municipal planning. Watch whether Løkke's price is housing deregulation or tax reform — both hit your wallet directly.

### EU POLITICS (cont'd): Orbán Is OUT — Magyar Wins Landslide
The April 12 Hungarian election delivered a seismic result: Péter Magyar's Tisza Party won 141 of 199 seats (53.6% of vote) — a two-thirds supermajority that allows constitutional amendments. Orbán's Fidesz collapsed to 55 seats (37.8%). Turnout hit 79.6%, the highest since 1990. Orbán conceded on election night. This ends 16 years of Fidesz rule and removes the EU's most persistent veto blocker on Ukraine aid, rule-of-law enforcement, and joint defense spending.

**Life Impact: HIGH** — Magyar's supermajority means Hungary can unfreeze billions in EU funds, stop blocking Ukraine support, and rejoin EU consensus. This accelerates EU-wide defense and migration policy that Denmark will need to engage with. The shift also strengthens the EU's negotiating position globally.

### MIDDLE EAST (cont'd): Iran Ceasefire Deal on Trump's Desk
A 60-day MOU has been negotiated between US and Iranian teams, but Trump hasn't signed. Key terms: Strait of Hormuz declared "unrestricted" with Iran removing all mines within 30 days; US naval blockade lifted proportionally as shipping resumes; sanctions waivers allowing Iranian oil sales; Iran commits not to pursue nuclear weapons with enriched uranium disposal as first negotiation topic. However, US and Iranian forces exchanged fire on May 29, and 115 commercial vessels remain redirected under the US blockade. Trump held a Situation Room meeting May 29 for "final determination."

**Life Impact: HIGH** — If Trump signs, oil prices should drop noticeably within weeks, easing energy costs across Europe. If he doesn't, continued blockade keeps prices elevated. Either way, the Hormuz situation has been the single biggest driver of fuel price volatility this year.

### DENMARK PROPERTY TAX: Valuations Hiked by Law Change — 1B DKK Revenue Grab
A legislative correction in the Folketing increased property valuations for owners in 81 municipalities, fixing what the government calls a "technical legal error" from 2023 legislation. Property value tax is 0.51% up to 9,007,000 DKK and 1.4% above that. The Ministry of Taxation estimates this adds over 1 billion DKK in state revenue over two years. You're still on provisional housing tax in 2026 pending final assessments.

**Life Impact: VERY HIGH** — If you own property (and from your emails, you're involved with E/F Fionia hus II and Tjørnholmvej 40-58 ApS), this directly increases your tax bill. The "technical error" framing is politically convenient but the money comes out of your pocket. Check your preliminary assessment carefully — you can still appeal the historical valuations (your dad forwarded you the Vurderingsstyrelsen complaint info).
