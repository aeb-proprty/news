### DANISH POLITICS: Frederiksen Forms Centre-Left Minority Coalition — Venstre in Opposition
UPDATE (previously: coalition talks stalled after March 24 snap election). After ten weeks of negotiations, Frederiksen announced on June 1 that she had formed a minority coalition with the Socialist People's Party, Radikale Venstre, and the Moderates — her third consecutive government. King Frederik formally appointed the cabinet on June 3. The four parties hold just 82 of 179 seats and rely on Enhedslisten for support. Frederiksen described the programme as being "for the people who live in Denmark now, for the generations to come, and also for the animals." Your party, Venstre, is in opposition. Top priorities: Greenland sovereignty standoff with Trump, defense buildup, cost-of-living, and animal welfare.

**Life Impact: HIGH** — Tax policy, housing regulation, and defense spending will be shaped by this coalition. The reliance on Enhedslisten for support means left-leaning economic and climate policies are likely. Venstre in opposition means limited influence on the agenda.

### IRAN/MIDDLE EAST: Ceasefire MOU Signed, Oil Prices Crash From $120 to $84 — But Hormuz Tensions Remain
UPDATE (previously: Day 38 of conflict, Iran rejected ceasefire). The war that began February 28 reached a ceasefire April 7-8. On June 17, Trump and Iranian President Pezeshkian signed a memorandum of understanding with a 60-day framework to end the conflict. Key terms: suspension of US sanctions on Iranian crude, release of ~$24B in frozen Iranian funds, Iran commits to no nuclear weapons development, Strait of Hormuz reopened to commercial traffic, US naval blockade withdrawn. Brent crude dropped from a wartime peak of ~$120/barrel to ~$84. However, Iran is now charging "service fees" for Hormuz passage and retains effective deterrence capability over the strait regardless of the deal.

**Life Impact: HIGH** — Oil prices dropping from $120 to $84 directly affects energy costs, fuel prices, and inflation across Europe. But the deal is fragile — the 60-day window expires mid-August, and Iran's "service fees" on Hormuz signal the crisis may not be over.

### EU: New €3 Customs Duty on E-Commerce Parcels Took Effect Yesterday
Starting July 1, 2026, the EU now charges a €3 customs duty per item on low-value parcels (up to €150) imported from outside the EU. The fee is per distinct product category in each parcel — so a package with a silk blouse and two wool blouses = €6. This is separate from VAT and applies to ~93% of e-commerce imports. The measure runs until July 2028. A second €2 customs handling fee is expected from November 2026.

**Life Impact: HIGH** — If you order from AliExpress, Temu, Shein, or any non-EU e-commerce, every order just got more expensive. Combined with the upcoming November fee, small orders from China become significantly less attractive.

**Life Impact: MEDIUM** — The Vortek system is the biggest multi-material advancement in consumer 3D printing in years. If you're considering an upgrade from your Creality setup (they're offering 44% off the K1 right now per that email), the Bambu H2C sets a new standard.
