### DANISH POLITICS UPDATE: Coalition Talks Enter Week 6, Frederiksen Pivots Right
The coalition deadlock continues. Key development: Moderates' Lars Løkke Rasmussen blocked the red-bloc coalition on April 10 over policy disputes, forcing Frederiksen to pivot toward right-leaning parties. She met with the Liberals (Venstre) and Conservatives this week. Moderates rejoined talks on April 22, but experts now expect no resolution until May. Denmark remains under caretaker government — the longest formation period in recent memory.

**Life Impact: HIGH** — Caretaker government means policy paralysis on housing, defense spending, and Greenland strategy. The longer this drags, the more market uncertainty for Copenhagen property and infrastructure decisions.

**Life Impact: HIGH** — The Hormuz standoff continues to pressure global oil/energy prices. UAE leaving OPEC could reshape oil markets. The War Powers deadline is the most significant political development — if Congress blocks, the conflict dynamics change entirely. Watch energy bills.

### AI: Claude Mythos 5 Launches (10T Parameters), GPT-5.5 Released, Hallucination Trap Discovered
Anthropic released Claude Mythos 5 — the first widely recognized 10-trillion-parameter model, built for cybersecurity and complex coding. Access is being restricted after it demonstrated the ability to identify and exploit software vulnerabilities in 80%+ of test cases. Adobe replaced Experience Cloud with CX Enterprise, an AI-first platform with persistent AI "Coworker" agents.

**Life Impact: MEDIUM-HIGH** — The hallucination-scales-with-capability finding is significant if you rely on AI agents for real work. Claude Mythos 5's vulnerability-finding capability raises serious security questions for the entire software industry. Adobe's shift signals enterprise AI adoption accelerating.

### 3D PRINTING: ELEGOO Jupiter 2 + CANVAS Multicolor Debut, Sub-$10K Metal Printer
ELEGOO officially launched two products at RAPID+TCT in Boston: the Jupiter 2 (14-inch 16K LCD resin printer, 20x26 micron XY resolution, shipping Q3 at $849 early bird) and the surprise debut of CANVAS, a multicolor system for the Centauri Carbon platform. Scrap Labs unveiled the Scrap 1 — a Laser Powder Bed Fusion metal 3D printer starting at $9,600 kit price, making metal AM accessible to prosumers for the first time. Anycubic also showed multicolor FDM and large-format resin at RAPID+TCT. Industry concern: potential material shortages ahead.

**Life Impact: LOW-MEDIUM** — The Jupiter 2 is relevant if you're considering large-format resin. The Scrap 1 metal printer at $9,600 is a milestone — metal 3D printing was previously $100K+. Material shortage warnings worth monitoring if you print regularly with your Creality.

## 2026-04-07

### DANISH POLITICS: Coalition Talks Drag On After Historic Election
The March 24 snap election left no bloc with a majority. Mette Frederiksen's Social Democrats won 38 seats but with their worst vote share (21.9%) since 1903. The Green Left surged to become the second-largest party (20 seats), and the Danish People's Party made significant gains. The Moderates are now kingmaker. Coalition negotiations are ongoing with no resolution yet. The Greenland/Trump crisis was the catalyst for the snap election and gave Frederiksen a rally-round-the-flag boost.

**Life Impact: HIGH** — A new government will directly affect tax policy, housing regulation, immigration rules, and Denmark's defense posture. If you're in Copenhagen, coalition outcomes on housing and green policy matter immediately.

### EU POLITICS: Parliament Simplifies AI Rules, Bans Nudifier AI
The European Parliament agreed on proposals to simplify the EU AI Act's application dates for high-risk systems and voted to ban AI "nudifier" systems outright. Separately, Parliament and Council reached agreement on a major EU Customs Code reform targeting e-commerce and product safety. New EU-wide anti-corruption criminal law framework was also adopted.

**Life Impact: MEDIUM-HIGH** — The AI rule simplification directly affects anyone working with or building AI systems in the EU. The customs reform will affect online shopping from non-EU sellers.

### COPENHAGEN HOUSING: Prices Jump 20% YoY, Seller's Market Deepens
