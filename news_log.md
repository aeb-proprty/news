### GEOPOLITICS: US-Iran Ceasefire Collapses, Major Escalation Underway
The June 18 ceasefire between the US and Iran has fallen apart. Iran attacked three commercial tankers in the Strait of Hormuz on July 6-7, prompting US strikes on 170+ Iranian military targets including air defenses, command networks, coastal radar, and 60+ IRGC boats. Iran's IRGC retaliated by hitting 85 US military targets in Bahrain and Kuwait. At the NATO summit in Ankara, Trump declared the ceasefire "over." Oil prices spiked ~4.4% in a single session, with Brent crude around $73.50/barrel. The Strait of Hormuz carries ~20% of global oil trade.

**Life Impact: HIGH** — Energy prices will rise across Europe. If this continues to escalate, expect fuel and heating cost increases in Denmark this autumn. Watch your energy bills.

### EU POLITICS: Air Passenger Rights Overhaul, EU-Mexico Trade Deal, AI Cybersecurity Plan
Three significant EU developments this week:

1. **Air passenger rights overhaul** (voted July 7, 646-12): Airlines must allow one free cabin bag, children under 14 seated next to parents at no charge, 30-day compensation payment deadline, 9-month claim window. Updates 2004 rules. Council confirmation still needed.
2. **EU-Mexico trade deal approved** (July 8, 479-119): Replaces the 2000 framework. Covers customs, IP, public procurement, sustainable development. Goes to Council, then ratification by all member states.
3. **EU Cybersecurity & AI Action Plan** (July 7): Requires advanced AI models to undergo risk assessment before EU market entry. Creates secure testing platform via ENISA. Also note: EU AI Act Article 50 transparency obligations take effect **August 2** — mandatory AI-content marking, deepfake labeling, and user notification.

**Life Impact: MEDIUM-HIGH** — The air passenger rights changes directly improve your travel experience. The AI Act transparency deadline on August 2 matters if you build or deploy AI in the EU.

### AI: GPT-5.6 and Grok 4.5 Launch on Same Day; Anthropic Overtakes OpenAI on Revenue
July 9 was the most consequential single day in AI model history:

- **OpenAI released GPT-5.6** in three tiers: Sol (flagship, $5/$30 per million tokens, 91.9% Terminal-Bench), Terra ($2.50/$15), and Luna ($1/$6). New naming convention replaces old system. Sol runs on Cerebras at 750 tokens/sec. The release was delayed weeks after the Trump administration requested early security review access — OpenAI publicly pushed back.
- **SpaceXAI launched Grok 4.5** the same day. 1.5T parameters, trained on Cursor session data (SpaceX is acquiring Cursor for $60B). Priced aggressively at $2/$6 per million tokens — 60% cheaper than Opus 4.8. Strong coding benchmarks: 62.0% DeepSWE (vs. Opus 4.8's 55.75%). Not yet available in the EU (expected mid-July).
- **Anthropic has overtaken OpenAI on revenue**: $47B annualized (May 2026), now profitable. OpenAI at $25-33B with $14B operating losses. Claude Code was the primary driver. Anthropic also signed a $19B, 20-year data center lease with TeraWulf (nuclear/hydro-powered) and is targeting an October 2026 IPO.
- **Fable 5 moved to paid billing** ($10/$50 per million tokens) and now requires government-issued ID verification for access, alongside Mythos 5. Export control compliance measure.
- **Chinese AI models now account for 30-46% of US enterprise API token usage** (up from 4.5% in H1 2025). Z.ai's GLM-5.2 scored 62.1% on SWE-bench Pro at a fraction of Western model costs.

**Life Impact: HIGH** — The AI pricing war is accelerating dramatically. Five frontier-class API tiers now compete, with pricing from $0.44 to $50 per million output tokens. If you build on AI APIs, this is a major cost and capability shift. Grok 4.5 EU availability imminent.

### AI SECURITY: First Autonomous AI Ransomware Attack Documented
Sysdig's Threat Research Team published analysis of "JadePuffer" — an AI-driven ransomware operation that autonomously executed a complete attack lifecycle. A human selected targets, but the AI agent ran everything: initial access via a Langflow RCE vulnerability (CVSS 9.8), 600+ payloads, self-corrected errors in 31 seconds, encrypted 1,342 database items. Five Eyes intelligence alliance separately warned that frontier AI models capable of sophisticated cyberattacks are "months, not years" away.

**Life Impact: MEDIUM** — This changes the cybersecurity threat landscape. If you manage infrastructure or property tech systems, autonomous AI attacks are now a real, documented threat — not theoretical.

### 3D PRINTING: Prusa INDX Kit Shipping, Volumetric Printing Goes Open-Source
- **Prusa INDX Conversion Kit** for CORE One/+ is entering production. First units leave the factory by end of July, full first batch shipping by end of August. The multi-tool system produces dramatically less waste (29g vs. up to 696g in competitors), supports up to 8 tools in CMYKW+RGB, and includes a new ColorMix halftoning feature. Installation: ~5-6 hours. Firmware 6.6.1 released with new PrusaSlicer profiles. Also: **MK4S flash summer sale (20% off) ends July 14.**
- **Volumetric 3D printing goes open-source**: The OpenCAL V2 project makes Computed Axial Lithography accessible to hobbyists — curing entire objects simultaneously in resin in minutes rather than hours. Now has user-friendly "Tomo" software and is working with Formlabs on affordable resins.
- **Creality K3** with KliTek nozzle-changing technology expected Q3 2026 (7x TPU flow rate of typical consumer printers). Creality also completed its Hong Kong stock exchange listing.

**Life Impact: MEDIUM** — The Prusa INDX is a significant upgrade if you own a CORE One. MK4S sale ending in 4 days. Volumetric printing is early but could be the next FDM-style revolution.
