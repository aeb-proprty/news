## 2026-07-09

### IRAN-US WAR: Ceasefire Collapses, US Strikes 80+ Targets — Oil Crisis Deepens
**Update from April 7 coverage:** The conflict has escalated dramatically since we last covered it at Day 38. Supreme Leader Khamenei was killed in earlier strikes. A memorandum of understanding (ceasefire) was reached but collapsed on July 7 when Iran attacked commercial ships in the Strait of Hormuz. The US retaliated on July 8 with strikes on "over 80 targets" across Iran, hitting Sirik, Qeshm, and Bandar Abbas. Trump declared the MoU "over" from the NATO summit in Ankara. Iran then struck targets in Bahrain, Kuwait, and Qatar in crossfire that threatens to drag Gulf states in. US reimposed sanctions on Iranian oil effective July 7. This is now the largest supply disruption in global oil market history.

**Life Impact: VERY HIGH** — Oil prices and energy costs are directly affected. If you drive, heat, or buy anything shipped by truck, you feel this. The Strait of Hormuz disruption affects ~20% of global oil transit. Watch fuel and electricity prices closely.

### NATO SUMMIT: Trump Meets Zelenskyy in Ankara, Ukraine Gets Patriot License
NATO summit wrapped in Ankara (Turkey). Trump met Zelenskyy and announced the US will license Ukraine to manufacture Patriot missiles domestically — a significant shift from just supplying them. This deepens US commitment to Ukraine while also being a way for Trump to frame it as "business" rather than aid. European defense buildup continues to accelerate.

**Life Impact: MEDIUM-HIGH** — Denmark's new government is directly involved in ramping up defense spending. EU/NATO defense posture affects Danish budgets, taxes, and strategic priorities. The Patriot license signals longer-term Western commitment to Ukraine.

### AIRBNB: New 15.5% Host-Only Fee Now Universal
**Directly relevant to you as an Airbnb host:** Airbnb has completed the global rollout of its simplified fee structure. All hosts now pay a single 15.5% service fee on the booking subtotal (nightly rate + cleaning + extra guest fees). The old split-fee model (3% host / 14% guest) is gone. Guests now see no Airbnb service fee line — they pay exactly the price you set. If you use property management software, you were switched on April 13. The email you received on July 8 about "Vi forenkler servicegebyrerne" is about this change. UK hosts had their final deadline on June 22.

**Life Impact: HIGH** — This directly affects your Airbnb revenue. The net effect depends on your pricing strategy. If you were on the old split fee, your effective cost went from 3% to 15.5%, but guests no longer see a separate fee, which can improve conversion. You likely need to adjust your pricing to account for the higher host fee.

### COPENHAGEN HOUSING: Prices Now DKK 75k/m², Up 14% YoY — Still a Seller's Market
**Update from April 7 coverage:** Apartment prices have continued climbing. Average apartment price is now ~DKK 75,000/m² (was ~66,000/m² average in April). A standard 80m² apartment costs about DKK 6 million (~€803,000). Prices are up 14% year-over-year in nominal terms. Nykredit forecasts 13.7% growth for 2026 total, slowing to 3.1% in 2027. Supply is at a record low, especially in central Copenhagen, while trading activity has increased. Rents up ~3% YoY. The market remains firmly a seller's market with shrinking inventory.

**Life Impact: VERY HIGH** — Whether buying or renting, the squeeze continues. The 14% YoY increase outpaces wage growth significantly. The forecast moderation to 3.1% in 2027 suggests the peak growth rate may be passing, but absolute prices keep rising.

### INNOVATION: Sodium-Ion Batteries Arrive, Quantum Entanglement in Visible Objects
Sodium-ion batteries (made from salt — cheap and abundant) are emerging as a viable alternative to lithium for grid storage and affordable EVs. Direct lithium extraction technology has been proven in Argentina and California, processing brine in hours instead of months. A centimeter-sized crystal showed clear quantum entanglement — demonstrating quantum behavior in everyday-scale objects for the first time. Magnon lifetimes extended to 18 microseconds (100x improvement), advancing quantum computing hardware. China tested CO₂-based rocket propulsion, pointing toward cleaner, cheaper space access.

**Life Impact: MEDIUM** — Sodium-ion batteries could significantly reduce EV and energy storage costs within 2-3 years. The quantum breakthroughs are foundational science that will take longer to reach consumers.

**Life Impact: HIGH** — A new government will directly affect tax policy, housing regulation, immigration rules, and Denmark's defense posture. If you're in Copenhagen, coalition outcomes on housing and green policy matter immediately.
