## 2026-06-04

### DANISH POLITICS: Frederiksen Forms "Four-Leaf Clover" Government After Record 69-Day Talks
After historically long negotiations — the longest in Danish history at 69 days — Mette Frederiksen secured her third term as PM on June 1. The new centre-left minority government pairs the Social Democrats with SF (Green Left), Radikale Venstre (Social Liberals), and Moderaterne (Moderates). Key domestic priorities include youth housing access, free public transport for under-22s, and housing taxation reform. Jacob Mark (SF) becomes Minister for Children, Older People and Housing. The cabinet faces an immediate foreign policy crisis over Greenland, with Frederiksen firmly rejecting any US annexation, stating it would "signal the end of NATO."

**Life Impact: VERY HIGH** — You sit on a housing board (E/F Fionia hus II). The new government's explicit focus on housing taxation reform and youth housing could directly affect property regulation, ejerforening obligations, and Copenhagen apartment values. Watch Jacob Mark's first moves closely.

### EU POLITICS: Parliament Clears EU-US Turnberry Trade Deal — Final Vote June 16
The European Parliament's trade committee approved the Turnberry deal on June 2. Under it, the EU removes customs duties on most US industrial goods while US tariffs on EU goods are capped at 15%. Key safeguards: a sunset clause (expires Dec 31, 2029), a snap-back provision letting the Commission suspend the deal if the US doesn't lift steel/aluminium tariffs by end of 2026, and anti-surge protections if American goods flood EU markets. Final plenary vote is June 16.

**Life Impact: MEDIUM-HIGH** — Consumer goods prices, trade flows, and Danish export industries will be affected. The asymmetry (EU drops duties, US keeps 15%) is controversial — the snap-back clause is the safety net.

### AI: GitHub Copilot Switches to Token-Based Billing — Developers Report 10-50x Cost Increases
On June 1, GitHub Copilot moved from flat-rate subscriptions to token-based billing. Every plan now includes a monthly AI Credit allotment, with charges based on input/output/cached token consumption. Developer backlash is intense: reports of monthly bills jumping from $29 to $750, or $50 to $3,000 for heavy users. Agentic sessions are hit hardest. The fallback model was removed. Microsoft concurrently announced MAI-Code-1-Flash and Project Polaris (its own coding model replacing GPT-4 Turbo as Copilot's default starting August 2026).

**Life Impact: HIGH** — If you use GitHub Copilot or AI coding tools, your costs may spike dramatically. Worth auditing your usage immediately. Microsoft's push to replace OpenAI models with in-house alternatives is also a strategic shift affecting the entire developer ecosystem.

### 3D PRINTING: California Passes AB 2047 — Mandating Firearm Detection Algorithms in All 3D Printers
California's Assembly approved a bill requiring every 3D printer sold in the state to embed a "firearm blueprint detection algorithm" that scans STL/CAD/G-code files before printing. By July 2027, the CA DOJ must publish detection standards; certification opens Jan 2028; compliance required by July 2028. The EFF has strongly opposed it, calling it censorship of digital manufacturing. The bill still needs Senate approval and Newsom's signature. New York passed similar legislation.

**Life Impact: MEDIUM** — Even outside the US, this sets a precedent. If these laws spread to the EU, they could fundamentally change how 3D printers work — adding mandatory file scanning to consumer devices. Worth watching.
