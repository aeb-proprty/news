### COPENHAGEN HOUSING: Nationalbanken Warns Market Is "Fundamentally Unsustainable"
*Continuing from Apr 7 — prices were 62,900 DKK/m2, up 20% YoY.*

Prices have climbed further to 75,000-76,000 DKK/m2 as of June 2026. A standard 80 m2 Copenhagen apartment now costs ~6 million DKK. On May 26, Nationalbanken director Ulrik Nodgaard issued a formal warning: prices are growing 20%+ annually while incomes grow 3-4%, calling it "grundlaeggende uholdbart" and warning it's something "we may all come to regret." Suburbs are surging even harder — Glostrup +39.5%, Albertslund +32%, Herlev +30.6% over two years. Variable mortgage rates jumped 0.35 percentage points in March due to the Iran war. The government is raising the cap on public housing (20,000-25,000 new units over the next decade) and there's growing political support for rent ceilings on the 200,000+ privately rented apartments built after 1991. Nykredit forecasts growth slowing to 3.1% in 2027 after 13.7% for full-year 2026.

**Life Impact: VERY HIGH** — If you're on a building board and living in Copenhagen, the Nationalbanken warning is a signal the music may slow. The rent regulation debate could directly affect property values and rental dynamics for buildings like yours. Variable-rate mortgage holders already feeling the pinch.

### DANISH POLITICS: Frederiksen Forms Third Government After 69-Day Marathon
*Continuing from Apr 7 — coalition talks were ongoing after the March 24 snap election.*

It took a record 69 days, but Mette Frederiksen sealed a centre-left minority coalition on June 3. The government is Social Democrats (38) + Green Left/SF (20) + Moderates (14) + Social Liberals (10) = 82 of 179 seats. The Moderates initially blocked a left-dependent coalition on April 10, Venstre's Troels Lund Poulsen tried and failed to form a blue-bloc government (collapsed May 22), then Frederiksen closed the deal. Major policy moves: defense spending over 3% of GDP with a 50 billion DKK acceleration fund, target 5% by 2032. Conscription expanded to women (11 months, up from 4 — Denmark joins Norway and Sweden). Food VAT to be halved (and eliminated on fruits/vegetables) but not until 2028. Coffee and chocolate taxes abolished from July 1.

**Life Impact: HIGH** — The new government is your government for the foreseeable future. The defense ramp-up is the biggest fiscal shift in a generation. The food VAT cut will eventually save you money on groceries. Conscription expansion is historic.

### EU POLITICS: Magyar's Landslide Ends Orbán Era, EU-US Trade Deal Sealed, New Import Duty Live
*Continuing from Apr 7 — Magyar was polling neck-and-neck with Orbán, AI rules being simplified.*

Peter Magyar won a historic landslide on April 12 — 53.1% of the vote, 141/199 seats, a constitutional supermajority. Orbán's 16-year grip on Hungary is over. Magyar immediately lifted Hungary's veto blocking EU accession talks with Ukraine and Moldova, enabling the EU to formally open the first accession negotiations cluster on June 15.

EU-US trade deal sealed May 20: the EU eliminated all duties on US industrial goods from July 1; US tariffs on EU products capped at 15%. A safeguard mechanism lets the EU suspend if the US doesn't lift steel/aluminum tariffs by end of 2026. The deal runs through 2029.

From July 1, a new EUR 3 customs duty applies per distinct item type on low-value parcels (under EUR 150) from outside the EU — ending the duty-free threshold that let ~5.9 billion items/year from Temu, Shein, and AliExpress enter untaxed. Both platforms are building EU warehouses to sidestep it.

The EU AI Act reaches full application August 2. The "Omnibus VII" agreement on May 7 postponed high-risk AI obligations to December 2027. Five new joint EU defence projects proposed July 3 totaling ~EUR 190B by 2036.

**Life Impact: MEDIUM-HIGH** — The Temu/Shein/AliExpress customs duty is already live and will make cheap imports slightly more expensive. The EU-US trade deal provides stability. The AI Act deadline August 2 matters if proprty.ai uses AI in any EU-regulated context.
