### DANISH POLITICS: Frederiksen Forms Third Government — Women-Led, Left-Leaning Coalition
After 69 days of negotiations — the longest coalition formation in Danish history — Mette Frederiksen secured a third term as PM. The coalition includes the Social Democrats, SF, Radikale Venstre, and the Moderates, holding a slim parliamentary majority. Key policy priorities: halving VAT on all food and abolishing it entirely on fruit and vegetables, free dental care for all Danes by 2030, free public transport for under-22s, and a tax reform giving many households ~12,450 DKK/year in tax cuts. Migration policy remains "strict and decisive." Venstre is in opposition.

**Life Impact: VERY HIGH** — The VAT cuts on food will directly lower your grocery bill. The tax reform means more money in your pocket starting January. As a Venstre member, your party is now in opposition — this shapes the political landscape you're active in. The coalition's housing and green policies will directly affect Copenhagen.

### EU POLITICS: Migration Pact Goes Live June 12 — Biggest Asylum Reform in a Generation
The EU Pact on Migration and Asylum becomes fully operational on June 12, 2026. This is the most significant overhaul of European asylum law ever: binding solidarity mechanisms (21,000 relocations or EUR 420M contributions), new border screening procedures, faster asylum processing, and expanded safe-third-country rules. Denmark played a key role — the December 2025 Council deal was sealed under the Danish EU presidency. Denmark has opted into three components of the pact.

**Life Impact: HIGH** — As a Dane and someone interested in EU politics, this reshapes how the EU handles migration at its borders. Denmark's partial opt-in means some rules apply, others don't — worth watching how this plays out domestically.
