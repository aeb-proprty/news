## 2026-07-14

### IRAN/HORMUZ: Ceasefire Collapses, Full-Scale Fighting Resumes — Gulf States Under Attack
*Continuing from April 7 — major escalation since then.*

Since the last update: Supreme Leader Khamenei was assassinated in a US/Israeli strike. His son Mojtaba was named successor but has not appeared publicly (reportedly severely injured). A ceasefire was reached in June but collapsed over the core dispute: Iran insists on controlling Strait of Hormuz navigation; the US demands a return to free shipping.

**This week**: Three consecutive nights of US strikes across Iran (July 11-13), hitting Bushehr, Chah Bahar, Jask, Bandar Abbas, and Abu Musa — targeting air defenses, radar, missiles, and small boats. The US deployed one-way attack sea drones for the first time. Iran declared the Strait of Hormuz closed and launched retaliatory strikes against Bahrain, Kuwait, Qatar, UAE, Oman, and Jordan. Iranian missiles hit two UAE tankers (one crew member killed). Brent crude surged 9.6% to $83.30/barrel — the biggest single-day jump in six years. Trump notified Congress that "limited" military action has resumed.

**Life Impact: VERY HIGH** — Oil prices spiking directly affects energy costs, fuel, and inflation in Denmark. If Hormuz stays closed, expect sustained price increases on everything from transport to goods. This is the single biggest global economic risk right now.

### DANISH POLITICS: Frederiksen Confronts Trump Over Greenland at NATO Summit
*Continuing from April 7 — coalition formed, new confrontation.*

The coalition talks resolved June 3: Frederiksen leads a four-party minority government (Social Democrats, SF/Green Left, Radikale Venstre, and the Moderates) with 82 of 179 seats. Key policy: plans to abolish the middle and top-tier income taxes introduced for 2026; retirement age to increase gradually to 70 by 2040.

**New this week (July 7-8)**: At the NATO summit in Ankara, Frederiksen directly confronted Trump after he renewed demands to acquire Greenland, stating "Greenland is not for sale" and that Denmark is "ready to defend every inch of NATO, including our own territory." The confrontation comes after JD Vance visited Pituffik Space Base in March and accused Denmark of underinvesting in Greenland's security.

**Life Impact: HIGH** — The tax reform will directly affect your take-home pay. The Greenland confrontation signals continued geopolitical tension that could affect Danish defense spending, NATO relations, and Arctic policy.

### EU POLITICS: Meta Faces Billions in Fines; Ukraine Accession Accelerates; Orbán is Out
*Continuing from April 7 — Hungary prediction came true, major new developments.*

**Meta / DSA (July 10)**: The European Commission found Meta in breach of the Digital Services Act for "addictive design" — specifically infinite scroll, autoplay, push notifications, and hyper-personalized recommendations on Instagram and Facebook. Meta faces fines up to 6% of global revenue (over $12 billion). The Commission recommended disabling autoplay and infinite scroll and adding screen-time breaks.

**Ukraine (July 14)**: The General Affairs Council opened Cluster 6 (External Relations) for Ukraine's EU accession negotiations after all 27 member states agreed. This follows Cluster 1 opening on June 15. Crucially: Hungary's new government (took power in May after Orbán lost — as flagged in the April update) dropped the veto on Ukraine's accession.

**Parliament (July 6-9, last before recess)**: Updated air passenger rights (compensation after 3-hour delay, fee-free child seating). EU-Mexico trade agreement voted on. Ireland took over the rotating Council presidency July 1.

**Life Impact: MEDIUM-HIGH** — The Meta ruling could change how Instagram and Facebook work for you within months. Ukraine's accession path reshapes EU spending and defense priorities. The passenger rights update is immediately useful next time you fly.

### COPENHAGEN HOUSING: Nationalbanken Warns of Bubble Risk — But Momentum Is Slowing
*Continuing from April 7 — prices higher, but signs of cooling.*

Prices have continued climbing to ~75,000 DKK/m² average (up from ~63,000 in April). But the monthly rate of increase has slowed to its lowest since May 2024. Nationalbanken issued a formal warning in May: prices surged 25% in 12 months through April, flagging signs of speculative, expectation-driven buying and stressing the need for sound credit standards.

Supply is finally rising: 1,630 apartments for sale in Copenhagen municipality at the start of July — up 7.4% month-over-month and 25% higher than a year ago, the highest level in 18 months. Fastest-rising neighborhoods: Sydhavn, Valby, and Nordvest (7-12% YoY). The stricter environmental building regs from the April update continue to constrain new construction.

**Life Impact: VERY HIGH** — If you're looking at the Copenhagen market, the Nationalbanken warning is significant — it's the closest to a bubble warning they've issued. But rising supply and slowing momentum may signal a turning point. Whether you're buying or holding, watch Q3 closely.

### 3D PRINTING: Bambu Lab Overtakes Creality as World's #1 Desktop Brand
*Continuing from April 7 — major market shift.*

In the first four months of 2026, nearly every second desktop 3D printer sold came from Bambu Lab vs. one in four from Creality. Overall printer sales up 59% YoY. Bambu Lab also unveiled the H2C — a dual-nozzle machine with a Vortek nozzle swapper that eliminates waste "poop" from multi-material printing. The 4th anniversary sale is running now through July 15 with significant discounts.

Creality filed for a third Hong Kong Stock Exchange IPO attempt in March amid mounting pressure. They launched "CubeMe," an AI tool that converts photos into 3D-printable chibi figures. Elegoo completed a 500M yuan ($73.3M) B+ funding round backed by Meituan and DJI, and launched the Centauri Carbon 2 Combo (four-color, 350C nozzle). Rolls-Royce opened a new additive manufacturing facility in Bristol for next-gen aircraft engine components.

**Life Impact: LOW-MEDIUM** — If you have a Bambu printer, the anniversary sale is immediately relevant. The H2C's waste elimination is a genuine quality-of-life improvement for multi-material printing. The market shift to Bambu may affect future ecosystem choices.

### GLOBAL HEALTH: Ebola Epidemic Becomes Third Largest on Record
The 2026 Ebola outbreak (Bundibugyo virus) in the DRC has reached 1,926 confirmed cases and 702 deaths as of July 11. It has spread substantially faster than any previous outbreak, surpassing 1,000 cases within 40 days (vs. 235 days in 2018). It has reached Uganda's capital Kampala. A US humanitarian worker was evacuated to Germany on July 13. There is no approved vaccine or treatment for this strain.

**Life Impact: MEDIUM** — No direct threat in Europe yet, but this is the fastest-spreading Ebola outbreak ever. Worth monitoring for potential travel advisories and global health response implications.
