### DANISH POLITICS: Troels Lund Poulsen Takes Over as Royal Investigator — Frederiksen Era Ends
UPDATE: Frederiksen's coalition talks collapsed May 8 when the Moderates withdrew support for her leadership. King Frederik X appointed Venstre leader Troels Lund Poulsen as royal investigator on May 11 with a 14-day deadline. Troels has excluded Enhedslisten, Alternativet, and SF (too far apart on economic policy — they want higher taxes, Venstre wants lower). Seven parties remain at the table: Social Democrats, Danish People's Party, Liberal Alliance, Moderates, Conservatives, Radikale Venstre, and Danmarksdemokraterne. Troels describes these as among the hardest negotiations he's been in. He emphasizes "personal questions" must be set aside. Deadline runs to approximately May 25.

**Life Impact: VERY HIGH** — This is the decisive phase. The next 10 days will determine Denmark's government, tax policy, housing regulation, defense spending, and green transition approach. Venstre's platform of lower taxes, stronger defense, and deregulation could directly affect your costs and the Copenhagen housing market.

### AI: The Jobs Apocalypse Debate Explodes
The Economist's cover story this week — "Prepare for an AI Jobs Apocalypse" — argues governments should build safety nets now, not wait for proof. They cite Danish-style flexicurity as one model and float partial nationalization of AI firms. Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei warned 50% of entry-level white-collar jobs (tech, legal, consulting, finance) could be wiped out within 5 years, with unemployment potentially hitting 20%. Counterpoint: a16z and Scott Galloway both argue it's "unhelpful marketing" — studies show AI isn't yet affecting employment rates, and only 2.5% of tasks match human-level performance. The debate is fierce and unresolved.

**Life Impact: HIGH** — This isn't abstract anymore. The Economist specifically references Denmark's flexicurity model. Whether or not the apocalypse comes, governments are now planning for it, which means policy changes (training programs, safety nets, possibly AI taxes) that will affect your working life.
