### MIDDLE EAST: Iran War Ends — US and Iran Reach Deal, Strait of Hormuz Reopening
**Continuation from Apr 7 — major development.** On April 7 the conflict was at Day 38 with escalating strikes and Iran rejecting a ceasefire. Since then: a brief ceasefire in April collapsed, Israel and Iran traded their worst strikes in months in early June (June 7-8), but yesterday (June 14) mediators announced a memorandum of understanding to be signed June 19, intended to formally end the conflict within 60 days. Trump announced the US is lifting its naval blockade on Iranian ports and the Strait of Hormuz will reopen. The war killed thousands in Iran and Lebanon, dozens in Israel and the Gulf states, and displaced millions. Supreme Leader Khamenei was killed in the opening salvo on Feb 28. This is the single biggest geopolitical shift in months.

**Life Impact: VERY HIGH** — Oil prices should stabilize and potentially drop as the Strait of Hormuz reopens. Fuel and energy costs in Denmark/EU should ease. Global recession fears tied to the conflict should recede. Watch pump prices over the next 2-4 weeks.

### AI: US Government Forces Anthropic to Kill Its Most Powerful Models
Anthropic launched Claude Fable 5 and Claude Mythos 5 on June 9 — a new "Mythos" tier above Opus. Three days later (June 12), the US government issued an export control directive requiring Anthropic to suspend all access to both models by any foreign national, inside or outside the US, including Anthropic's own employees. Since Anthropic can't filter by nationality in real-time, both models were shut down entirely for everyone. This is the first government-forced takedown of a publicly deployed frontier AI model. All other Claude models (including Opus 4.8) remain available.

**Life Impact: HIGH** — This sets a precedent for government control over AI access. As a non-US user, you're directly affected — locked out of the most capable models. This could fragment the AI ecosystem along national lines and reshape how AI companies deploy globally. EU regulation may accelerate in response.

### EU POLITICS: Mandatory Foreign Investment Screening + Safeguards Against Future Orbáns
The EU adopted a new Foreign Direct Investment screening regulation (June 8) requiring all 27 member states to screen foreign investments in critical sectors — energy, transport, digital infrastructure, critical raw materials, and electoral systems. Closes the loophole where non-EU investors used EU subsidiaries to bypass screening. Member states have 18 months to comply. Separately, Germany, France, Netherlands, Belgium, and Luxembourg are pushing stronger safeguards to act faster against democratic breaches in future EU member states — directly inspired by the Orbán experience.

**Life Impact: MEDIUM** — The FDI screening won't affect you day-to-day, but the democratic safeguards proposal matters for EU stability and could affect future enlargement politics. The innovation platform for startups launched this week could be relevant if you're building tech.
