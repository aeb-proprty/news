### DANISH POLITICS: Troels Lund Poulsen Takes Over Government Formation — Frederiksen Out
Mette Frederiksen's 6-week attempt to form a coalition has collapsed. The Moderates pulled out of talks late last week, and the King handed the mandate to Venstre leader Troels Lund Poulsen on Friday May 9. Poulsen met all Folketing parties plus the North Atlantic mandates on Day 1 (Sunday May 11) — you got his newsletter about it. He has a two-week deadline to form a government. His tone was conciliatory — inviting both red and blue blocs — but he stressed "responsible economic policy" as a prerequisite, signaling Venstre will push for economic reforms as the price of coalition entry. Every scenario is difficult: a pure blue-bloc majority is razor-thin and depends on parties with conflicting demands.

**Life Impact: VERY HIGH** — This is the most consequential Danish political shift since the snap election. A Venstre-led government would likely mean different housing regulation, tax policy, and defense spending priorities than a Frederiksen III cabinet. Directly affects Copenhagen housing rules and your business environment.

### MIDDLE EAST: Iran War Day 72 — Peace Talks Stall, Trump Rejects Iran Counter-Offer

**Life Impact: HIGH** — Strait of Hormuz remains partially disrupted, keeping global energy prices elevated. A failed peace deal means continued economic drag. Oil and shipping costs affect everything from groceries (your nemlig.com deliveries) to business operating costs.

### COPENHAGEN HOUSING: Nykredit Forecasts 13.7% Price Rise in 2026
Continuing the trend from last month: Copenhagen apartment prices remain at ~62,900 DKK/m² median. Nykredit now forecasts a 13.7% total rise for 2026, cooling to 3.1% in 2027. However, Realkredit Danmark offers a contrarian view — they expect a more subdued increase, arguing apartments have hit a price range where demand becomes more limited. Only 22% of Copenhagen housing is owner-occupied (extremely low for a European capital), meaning any demand increase hits a market with almost no supply flexibility. Average days-on-market: 14-21 days for well-located properties. Rental vacancy remains near 0% centrally.

**Life Impact: VERY HIGH** — You're actively searching on Boligsiden. The 13.7% annual forecast means every month of waiting costs you roughly 1% more. The Realkredit Danmark contrarian view suggests a possible plateau, but structural supply constraints (22% owner-occupied stock, near-zero vacancy) make a meaningful price drop unlikely.
